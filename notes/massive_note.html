1. Hypotheses (each committed to git before any option price for it was requested)

  • H-A, executive exits → cash-secured put (ceo/cfo/executive_officer_departure, pooled; commit dbbfe6c). A vivid, ambiguous exit makes holders overpay for downside puts (demand pressure, Gârleanu et al. 2009), while mega-cap businesses rarely change overnight. So the put seller earns an event premium beyond the ordinary variance risk premium.
  • H-B, CFO appointment → cash-secured put (workshop suggestion; HYPOTHESIS_5.md). Filling the CFO seat resolves financial-reporting uncertainty, but implied volatility mean-reverts with a lag, so sold puts collect the leftover premium. Pre-declared side tags: CEO appointment, and CFO departure (predicted no edge).

Spec = the notebook's defaults, so it matches what judges run: top-100 universe, 3–6m expiry bucket, entry at the close of the filing session, 5% OTM, fixed horizons, and the notebook's placebo (same tickers on ordinary days, 30 days or more from any event). The starter's API cell and pipeline cells run unchanged; we only set the window and the event table (exec_exits_8k.ipynb, EVENTS/START/END).

2. Method: three windows and a star rule fixed in advance

Options history reaches back to 2022, so we use three non-overlapping windows. For H-B: discovery 2022–23, confirmation 2024–25, final Jan–Aug 2026, run once. A cell is "starred" when the 95% bootstrap CI of events minus placebo excludes 0. H-B passes discovery only if the CSP is positive and starred at ≥2 of the 10/21/42-session/expiry horizons. For H-A (registered earlier): in-sample 2024–25, OOS Jan–Aug 2026 once. In total, 8 pre-registered trials are reported (TRIALS.md).

H-A fails. The put trade is about zero in 2024–25. In 2026 it turns positive only because exit stocks rallied +6.8–9.0% vs placebo, and a short put is long delta. That is regime luck, not the volatility premium. H-B fails. It has no stars in discovery. The nearly-starred expiry cell (+2.34%) was registered as a follow-up (trial 8, HYPOTHESIS_8.md) and tested on unseen 2024–25: −0.23%. The near-star was noise.

Starred cells vs chance (taking the workshop's advice seriously). At our pre-declared key horizons, 2022–23 has 0 starred of 97 cells (3 tags × top-100/tech × 5 strategies × 4 horizons), against 4.9 expected by chance. Across the notebook's full boards there are 8 of 135, against 6.75 expected, all at 1–5 sessions. Stars appear at the chance rate, so reading individual stars as signals would be p-hacking. The confirmation step shows what happens to the best-looking one. Large-cap tech (tip): only 6 CFO-appointment events in 2022–23, positive but uninformative.

4. What did replicate: executive exits move more than the options priced

Figure. |Realized| ÷ implied move (3–6m ATM straddle on the pre-event session, √t-scaled), mean and 95% CI.

After exits, realized moves exceed what the chain priced, relative to ordinary days, in both windows. 2024–25, 1–21 sessions: 1.00–1.13 vs placebo 0.80–0.92. 2026, 5–21 sessions: 1.28–1.43 vs 0.92–1.06 (21d: 1.43 [1.08, 1.83] vs 0.99 [0.83, 1.14]). The market prices an exit like an ordinary day. That is the opposite of the put-seller story, and it is why selling puts does not pay. Sensitivity (36 pre-declared variants: 1m/2m/3–6m × pre/post × 3/5/10% OTM × all exits vs CEO+CFO): 0 of 54 tradeable CSP cells are starred, and short-dated puts do worst (1m −0.54%), consistent with under-priced exit risk.

5. What would break it, how we would trade it, and our sealed-window predictions

Limits: small samples (14–163 priced events per window); a static 2026 top-100 list (survivorship); filing date ≠ announcement time (many exits are press-released first); daily last-trade marks, not NBBO mid. Trade implied by the replicated finding (stated, not tested, because it was not pre-registered): a delta-hedged long 1m ATM straddle on exit filers, entered at the filing-session close and held 5–21 sessions. Top-100 ATM options trade almost every session; at 1–2 contracts per event, ADV does not bind, and about 100 exits a year make it a small event sleeve. Hash-stamped predictions (FORWARD_LEDGER.md P1–P4, FORWARD_LEDGER_2.md P5–P7): on your window, exit and CFO-appointment CSPs will not beat placebo; exit realized/implied will exceed placebo; stars will appear at about the chance rate.
