# Evidence addendum to the acquisition-filer note

The historical idea is that acquisition overpayment and integration difficulties may hurt a buyer relative to the market. Short the buyer and buy a beta-scaled SPY hedge for 60 sessions. An acquisition-tagged filer is not necessarily the buyer, and an old deal mentioned in a new filing is not a new acquisition signal.

Thirty frozen stratified cases now have exact-accession, source-linked role and event-status reviews: 19 buyers, 10 targets and one complex combination. CRC's issuer is the buyer despite a passage calling another party “the Company.” WillScot's sampled filing announces termination of the earlier agreement. Fourteen cases would satisfy the new buyer/new-agreement filter. These are assistant reviews of licensed parsed SEC Items, without guaranteed exhibits or independent human adjudication. Agreement with heuristic suggestions is a diagnostic of this selected sample, not classifier accuracy.

A separate licensed-input reconstruction retrieved 4,080 responses and rebuilt the original 1,109-event universe and 715 price columns. Four daily-return series, historical metrics and 100 seeded placebos matched exactly. This supports reproducibility of the original computation; it cannot establish that vendor history was available at the historical decision time. The public ledger code and manual action journal were reused, so the exercise is not an independent software implementation.

The new forward protocol fixes admissions from October 5, 2026 through October 4, 2027, verified buyer/new-agreement eligibility, actual receipt timing, pre-close integer orders, dated locate/margin evidence and one primary cash-excess mean test with Newey-West lag 5. Evaluation waits for all 60-session exits; fewer than 30 entries or 200 reconciled sessions is inconclusive. Code, protocol and session calendar are locally hash-frozen. External timestamp anchoring and future licensed/operator records remain outstanding. No prospective Sharpe or return is claimed.

Historical portfolios and headline metrics are unchanged: the controlled historical test has 9.18% CAGR, 0.555 annualized cash-excess Sharpe, 9.89% annualized volatility and −9.19% maximum drawdown. Through August 2026, before the carried-forward September RF assumption, its cash-excess Sharpe is approximately −0.010. The examined historical window remains exploratory. Source labels and reproducible accounting strengthen the evidence without turning it into a pristine holdout.
