# Verified-buyer forward test, version 1

This protocol is developed using already examined historical evidence and the 30-case source audit. It is a NEW prospective hypothesis, not a retroactive preregistration of the original acquisition-filer results. Its exact freeze timestamp, code hashes and calendar hash are in `forward_freeze.json`. The local hash is tamper-evident only when compared with an independently saved copy; it is not an external registration or proof that GitHub received it. Commit/publish the freeze before the first admission if an external time anchor is required.

## One primary hypothesis and fixed observation horizon

New, binding acquisition agreements announced by verified listed buyers predict negative subsequent buyer performance relative to an entry-beta SPY hedge, after executable paper costs. Acquisitions can create overpayment and integration costs, but may also create value. No financing, momentum, size/style or deal-value optimization is allowed. Unknown consideration is retained; financing type is descriptive, not a filter.

Admissions begin October 5, 2026 and end October 4, 2027, using the published NYSE session calendar. Both SEC filing time and actual first observation must be after the freeze. Decisions must also fall within the admission window. All positions run to their fixed exit; the final evaluation occurs after the last admitted position's 60-session exit, not at a favorable interim peak. Fewer than 30 executed entries or 200 fully reconciled daily sessions makes the result inconclusive. The window is not extended to achieve significance. No prospective performance exists at freeze time.

The single primary statistic is mean daily net NAV return less the pre-observed cash benchmark, tested with Newey-West lag 5 standard errors. One-sided threshold t > 1.645 and positive mean excess return is specified before any prospective outcome. This threshold tests the new forward sample only, not the project-wide discovery family. Report annualized arithmetic return, CAGR, cash-excess Sharpe, volatility, drawdown, costs, skips, missing sessions and realized factor exposures. Factor attribution and cash/stock/industry splits are secondary descriptive results, with no threshold or alternate winner selection. Interim monitoring is allowed only for risk and data quality, without strategy changes or significance-driven stopping.

## Issuer identity and event status

Massive's acquisition-agreement disclosures are candidate discovery, not truth. Fetch the exact-accession 8-K Items and the relevant original exhibit when the Items are insufficient. Record accession, CIK, ticker, SEC URL, paragraph evidence, source SHA256, source received time, review time, reviewer and review method. Resolve the issuer against parties explicitly; the word Company may refer to the target in a buyer's filing. Wholly owned buyer subsidiaries count as issuer acquisitions.

Admit only `acquirer` plus `new_agreement`, meaning a newly announced, binding, not-yet-completed third-party business/equity/asset purchase. Reject targets, sellers, unknown or mixed/complex issuer roles, auction/stalking-horse bids, nonbinding offers, reverse-merger/Reverse Morris Trust combinations, completed deals, financing updates, proxy/regulatory updates, amendments and terminations. Duplicate acquisitions are deduplicated by CIK within 30 calendar days of the last recorded accepted decision. A risk fill failure does not permit relabeling the same event for a later profitable retry. Independent human signoff is required for ambiguous or complex evidence before any admission; otherwise skip. Assistant review is recorded as such and is not labeled human validation.

## Receipt timing and precommitted orders

Vendor tags are updated daily and can be loaded after filing. Earliest entry is a trading session whose date is strictly later than the filing's New York date AND the actual first-observed New York date. Source receipt, source review and decision times must be ordered and no later than order commitment. There is no backdating to a filing-day or prior-session close. The runner obtains observation and decision times from the local clock; future or backdated journal records fail verification.

Decision time is one hour before that session's published close: 15:00 New York normally, 12:00 on the listed 13:00 early closes. Within five minutes after that cutoff, use stock and SPY quote observations no more than 60 seconds old and available before the cutoff. Fixed integer share quantities are calculated from these pre-close quotes and decision-time NAV, then committed. Closing auction prices determine fills, not order size. A late/missing review or quote is skipped for that session; a separately logged operational delay must never be disguised as an earlier decision. An eligible first-session admission has no later-entry retry.

Beta uses only historical split-consistent daily observations in sessions [-260:-10], clipped to 0.5-1.5, fallback 1 if fewer than 120 paired observations. Save the input history and its latest-observed timestamp. Prior market cap must be at least $2B and prior 20-session dollar ADV must be available. These inputs must be observed before decision, with their measurement date no later than the previous session. No revised financial data or post-close NAV is substituted at decision time.

## Paper execution and risk

Initial paper NAV is $1M. At decision-time marks, cap stock exposure at 2% per issuer and 100% total short, with fee-aware entry sizing and prior ADV participation at most 5%. Reject concurrent issuer overlap. Obtain dated locate capacity, annualized borrow rate and broker margin approval before entry; absent or stale records block the primary paper admission. Locates and margin are not inferred from price data. There are no real-money orders in this package. The prospective planner consumes operator-supplied quote/locate/margin/state records; it does not invent a broker connection.

Stock costs are 10 bps and SPY costs 2 bps on actual filled notional, with fills/partial fills and additional broker fees recorded. Borrow uses the recorded broker quote and calendar days/365. Cash/debit financing and the cash benchmark use documented rates observed before the relevant accrual interval. Restricted short proceeds earn no assumed rebate. Dividends, splits, successor securities and contingent obligations require dated source/broker records. Unreconciled cash or action liabilities prevent a definitive primary result.

Order shares are fixed before close; post-fill price movement can breach decision caps. Log the breach and commit reductions at the next executable decision, using that time's quotes and costs. Do not fabricate a same-close corrective fill. Missing/stale quotes stop new entries. A borrow recall, broker margin failure or insolvency triggers an execution incident and earliest feasible cover. Record actual delay and resulting loss; do not remove these observations. The planner and timestamp journal are supplied, but a reconciled forward NAV ledger and broker records must be collected before claiming prospective performance.

## Reproducibility and amendments

`forward.py` freezes the protocol, calendar, planner, label rules and tests and creates an empty production journal. The journal uses chained SHA256 records, stores decisions before fills and rejects tampering against the saved freeze. A minimal timestamped observation/review/fill interface and deterministic adversarial tests are provided. All no-entry decisions and data gaps must be logged. Never overwrite a frozen file or a historical decision; later bug fixes require a new version and a dated incident, leaving version 1 results identifiable.

Independent historical reconstruction lives in an isolated workspace and records every licensed/public response, source URL, receipt time and hash. It rebuilds events, cap eligibility, prices, ADV, dividends, corporate actions and factors without parent derived inputs. It reuses the publicly supplied ledger; this is independent INPUT reconstruction, not an independent software implementation or blind holdout. Historical P&L and labels stay separate from the new forward test.

Unscheduled future exchange closures require an operational calendar amendment recorded before the affected decision; none is assumed known now.
